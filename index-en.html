About PlaceLog

PlaceLog detects arrivals and departures at registered places and automatically records your visit history and stay duration.

You can easily review the places you visit and your movement history, including business trips, commuting, travel, and other daily activities.

Features

  • Automatically detect arrivals at registered places
  • Detect departures from registered places
  • Automatically record stay duration
  • View visit and stay history
  • Departure reminders
  • Search registered places by name, address, or memo
  • View arrival time, departure time, and stay duration on the dashboard
  • View Movement History (Premium)
  • Export stay history as CSV (Premium)

Premium

Premium includes the following features:

  • Unlimited place registration
  • Unlimited stay history storage
  • CSV export of stay history
  • Movement History

Privacy

PlaceLog does not send location data, visit history, stay history, or movement history to external servers.

Recorded data is stored locally on your device and is not stored on external servers.

To use location-based features such as arrival and departure detection, stay history, and movement history, "Location Services: Always Allow" permission is required on your iPhone.
