About PlaceLog

PlaceLog detects arrivals at registered places and automatically records your visit history and stay duration.

You can easily review your daily movements, including business trips, commuting, travel, and other activities.

Features

  • Automatically detect arrivals at registered places
  • Automatically record stay duration
  • View visit history
  • CSV export
  • Store all data locally on your device

Privacy

PlaceLog does not collect personal information.

Location data and history records are stored on your device and are never sent to external servers.

To detect arrivals, "Location Services: Always Allow" permission is required on your iPhone.
