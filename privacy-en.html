Privacy Policy

PlaceLog

1. Information We Collect

PlaceLog uses location information to detect arrivals at registered places and record stay history.

2. Purpose of Use

Location information is used only for arrival detection, recording stay duration, and displaying history.

3. Data Transmission

PlaceLog does not send location information or usage history to external servers. All data is stored locally on your device.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

PlaceLog does not provide collected information to third parties.
