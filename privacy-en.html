Privacy Policy

PlaceLog

1. Information We Collect

PlaceLog uses location information to detect arrivals and departures at registered places and to record visit, stay, and movement history.

2. Purpose of Use

Location information is used to detect arrivals and departures at registered places, record visit and stay history, calculate stay duration, record movement history, and display this history within the app.

Location information is also used to provide departure reminders configured by the user.

3. Data Transmission

PlaceLog does not send location information, visit history, stay history, or movement history to external servers. This data is stored locally on your device and is not stored on external servers.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

PlaceLog does not provide collected information to third parties.
